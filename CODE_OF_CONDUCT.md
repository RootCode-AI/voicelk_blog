# Code of Conduct

VoiceLK welcomes contributors, readers and researchers of different backgrounds,
languages, identities, abilities and levels of experience. This code covers issues,
pull requests, reviews and other project-managed community spaces, including
public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and evidence respectfully; disagree with conclusions without
  attacking the person presenting them.
- Be patient with new contributors and people communicating in another language.
- Make room for accessibility needs and different communication styles.
- Give credit, respect consent, and protect private information.
- Accept corrections and help keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualised comments, unwanted sexual
attention, deliberate intimidation, impersonation and retaliating against a
reporter are not acceptable. Neither are publishing someone else's private
information without permission, repeated disruptive posting or continuing
unwanted contact after being asked to stop.

## Reporting concerns

Email **rootcode.projects@gmail.com** with the subject `VoiceLK conduct report`.
Include links, a brief account of what happened and any relevant evidence you are
comfortable sharing. Do not post private allegations or personal details in a
public issue. You may also use GitHub's reporting options where available.

If a concern involves a maintainer, identify that conflict and request review by
another available maintainer. GitHub's platform reporting channel remains an
independent option. This mailbox is not an emergency service.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers should review reports fairly, limit sharing to what is needed to
investigate and respond, and avoid disclosing a reporter's identity unnecessarily.
Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed, including when disclosure is
required by law or platform safety processes.

Depending on severity and context, maintainers may request a change in behaviour,
remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily or permanently,
or refer a matter to GitHub. Severe incidents need not receive a prior warning.
People affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration by emailing
the same address with relevant context. No fixed response time is promised.

Reports made in good faith must not lead to retaliation. Maintainers are expected
to follow this code as well as enforce it.
